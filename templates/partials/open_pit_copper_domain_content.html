Section 01

Executive Overview

Mining industry context and business drivers

Mining Industry Context

Open-pit copper mining is a large-scale surface extraction method used to recover copper ore from near-surface deposits. Operations involve removing overburden and waste rock to access ore bodies, which are then processed through crushing, grinding, and concentration stages to produce copper concentrate for downstream smelting and refining.

Cost Control

Productivity

Maximizing equipment utilization, throughput, and workforce efficiency.

Safety

Eliminating fatalities, reducing incidents, and maintaining regulatory compliance.

Sustainability

Managing water use, energy consumption, emissions, tailings, and community impact.

Grade Optimization

Controlling ore dilution and loss to maximize recoverable metal.
