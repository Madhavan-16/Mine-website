2.2 Evolution timeline

From sulfur origins on the Gulf Coast to a copper-first global miner — follow the spine through eight pivotal eras in Freeport's history.

  1. 1912
    Era 01

    The beginning

    Sulfur, not copper: the Freeport Sulphur Company and the Frasch process on the Texas Gulf Coast.

  2. 1913–1950s
    Era 02

    Diversification

    A holding model spanning gas, light, oil, and foreign mining: manganese, nickel, potash, and more.

  3. 1959–1972
    Era 03

    Indonesia & Ertsberg

    Dozy's 1936 discovery; rights in 1960; 1972 production and a 109 km slurry pipeline in New Guinea.

  4. 1967–1981
    Era 04

    McMoRan

    McMoRan Oil and Gas, Freeport Minerals, and the 1981 merger creating Freeport-McMoRan Inc.

  5. 1988
    Era 05

    Grasberg

    One of the great copper-gold systems—redefining Freeport as a world-scale miner.

  6. 2007
    Era 06

    Phelps Dodge

    A $25.9B combination adding Morenci and other assets among the world's top copper companies.

  7. 2012–2016
    Era 07

    Recentering

    Oil and gas build-out, then commodity stress—strategic return to the core copper business.

  8. 2018–2026
    Era 08

    Resilience and today

    Indonesian partnership renewal and lease to 2041; copper for AI, electrification, and mine productivity.

2.5 Key operations & assets

Site detail

Operating context by asset — open pit vs underground, leach vs concentrator — so delivery teams know where telemetry, historians, and production data originate.

Grasberg

Indonesia
Commodity focus
Copper & gold — underground block cave
Why it matters
World-scale ore body; ~14,000 ft elevation; Indonesian partnership and governance complexity.
Operating context
Advanced underground block-caving (transition from former open pit); concentrator/mill processing. Geotechnical, hoist, and mill throughput signals dominate analytics.

Americas

US · Peru · Chile
Commodity focus
Copper (primary); molybdenum at select sites — e.g. Morenci, Bagdad, Cerro Verde
Why it matters
~70% of US refined copper production linked to Freeport; largest contributor to Americas copper supply.
Operating context
Mix of large open-pit mines, leach/SX-EW pads, and concentrators — profile varies by site (Morenci: leach + mill; others concentrator-heavy). Fleet telematics, mill historians, and leach-pad instrumentation are primary data sources.

Atlantic Copper

Spain
Commodity focus
Smelting & refining — downstream value chain
Why it matters
Integrated path from concentrate to cathode; European refining hub in FCX's global network.
Operating context
Process-plant and metallurgical accounting — furnaces, anode/cathode tracking, emissions — distinct from mine-site OT and haul-fleet telemetry.
